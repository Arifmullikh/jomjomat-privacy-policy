Privacy Policy — Jomjomat: Merge & Have Fun

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Jomjomat: Merge & Have Fun (“the App”) does not directly collect, store, sell, or share users' personal information.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, physical address, precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

2. Offline Gameplay

The core gameplay of the App is designed to work offline.

The App does not operate a user account system, leaderboard, online multiplayer service, or its own backend for collecting user information.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

Payments are processed by Google Play. The App does not access users' full payment card numbers, bank account details, or other payment credentials.

The App may receive transaction-related information from Google Play, such as product information and purchase status, for the purpose of initiating, completing, consuming, and restoring in-app purchases.

The App does not transmit this purchase-related information to its own server or maintain a separate personal-information database.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Play Billing to process optional in-app purchases.

Google may process information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The App does not contain advertisements or third-party advertising SDKs.

The App does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or similar analytics services to collect user activity data.

6. Permissions

The Android application requests Internet access and Google Play Billing access as required for application functionality, including communication with Google Play for in-app purchases.

The App does not request sensitive permissions such as location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or a server-side database containing users' personal information.

Because the App does not maintain user accounts, there is no user account or personal-information database that requires an account deletion request.

Uninstalling the App generally removes app data stored locally on the device, subject to Android/device backup behavior.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Security

We do not maintain our own server-side collection of personal information.

In-app purchase transactions are handled through Google Play's payment infrastructure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the App's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated version will be made available at the same privacy-policy URL with a revised effective date.
